An expense splitter for a group of friends sharing a trip.
Users can add people to the group and remove people who have no expenses.
Anyone can record an expense: description, amount, who paid, and which people share it
(split equally, or by exact amounts that must add up to the total).
The system shows each person's balance (how much they are owed or owe).
The system suggests the minimum set of payments to settle all debts.
The system lists all expenses and lets a user delete an expense.
Amounts must be positive and use 2 decimal places; reject invalid splits with a clear message.
